**Who:** Marketplace sellers who manage 50+ product listings and update them every week.

**What they want:** Spend less time on listing chores; sell more; avoid mistakes that get listings rejected.

**What they don't care about:** Internal systems, refactors, ticket numbers, our team names.

**How to write for them**

- Lead with the benefit: what they can now do, or what got easier or faster.
- Plain words, short sentences, second person ("You can now…").
- One or two sentences per item. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- Mention where to find the feature if it's not obvious.
- Fixes: describe the problem they would have noticed, then that it's fixed.

**Example**

> **Edit many titles at once.** You can now select up to 100 listings and update their titles in one step, instead of
> opening each listing. Find it under *Listings → Bulk actions*.
