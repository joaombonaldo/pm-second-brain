# Acme Pricing Assistant

## Mission

Suggest competitive prices to marketplace sellers and explain each suggestion in plain words,
so they can react to the market without guessing.

## Current focus

Getting to beta: reliable price feed and clear explanations for every suggestion.

## What we're building

- Price elasticity model per category
- Price feed data quality audit

## Reusable assets

- Competitor price feed, daily, 5 categories (ask Bruno Reis)

## Needs

- Tested prompt templates for seller-facing copy (people/tool)
- Signal of upcoming demand per category (data)

## Recent decisions

- [decision] Beta waits until every suggestion has a plain-language explanation. — 2026-10-14
- [decision] Price suggestions stay advisory: sellers always confirm before a price changes. — 2026-10-07
